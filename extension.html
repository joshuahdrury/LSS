Famous Case Study: Taylor Swift 📝

Color-Coding & Voice Memos as Working Memory Supports

Did you know Taylor Swift writes all her lyrics using three distinct "pens" (Fountain Pen, Glitter Gel Pen, and Quill) depending on the mood of the song? She also records short voice notes on her phone the instant an idea strikes her.

Why this works: Her brain uses external systems (voice memos & visual colors) so her working memory doesn't get overloaded trying to remember musical ideas!
