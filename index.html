The Brain's CEO: Quick Recall

In our lesson, we learned that Executive Functioning is like the CEO of your brain. It controls 4 main areas:

🧠 Working Memory

Holding instructions and information in your mind while completing a task.

🛑 Impulse Control

Pressing the "brakes" on distractions or sudden reactions before thinking.

📋 Organization

Keeping track of school supplies, homework, assignments, and time.

🎯 Goal Planning

Breaking big long-term goals into smaller, manageable daily steps.
