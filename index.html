1. Quick Recap: Last Time We Met

During our first lesson, we learned that your brain isn't fixed—it grows stronger every time you try hard things. This is called Neuroplasticity.

Neurons & Pathways

Think of your brain like a forest. When you learn something new, you build a small trail. The more you practice, the wider and smoother that trail gets!

Mistakes are Signals

Making mistakes doesn't mean you aren't smart. It just means your brain is building new pathways. Keep pushing through!

2. The Brain's Control Center

Imagine your brain is a busy airport. Planes are taking off and landing everywhere. Who keeps everything organized so planes don't crash?

The Air Traffic Controller!

In your brain, this is called your Executive Function Skills. Located right behind your forehead (the Prefrontal Cortex), these skills help you plan, focus, and manage your time.

🧠 Working Memory

Holding onto information in your head while using it (like remembering a multi-step instruction from a teacher).

🛑 Impulse Control

Stopping yourself from reacting immediately (like waiting your turn to speak or ignoring your phone while doing homework).
