My Brain & Learning 🧠

Picture This...

“A teacher once told this kid's mother: 'Your son will never be able to focus on anything.'”

Years later, he became one of the most successful athletes in history with 28 Olympic medals (23 gold)!

It was Michael Phelps! 🏊‍♂️

Phelps was diagnosed with ADHD in sixth grade—the exact age that you are right now. As a child, he struggled to sit still and pay attention in class.

Building Systems for Success

Did Michael Phelps suddenly become good at focusing? Or did he build systems that helped his brain focus?

🏊‍♂️ Michael Phelps

Swimming gave him structure. His life became organized around practices, routines, repetition, clear goals, and precise preparation with his coach.

🤸‍♀️ Simone Biles

One of the greatest gymnasts in history didn't just practice gymnastics. Every year, she and her grandmother sat down to plan 12-month goals and short-term goals to assist her long-range ones.

Think-Pair-Share 💬

What helps successful athletes and learners succeed?

  • Having a routine and schedule
  • Setting clear goals & breaking big goals into smaller tasks
  • Repetition & practicing even when they don't feel like it
  • Having accountability and coaches/teachers to support them

Key Takeaway: Organization and time management aren't about being a "naturally organized person." They are about building systems that help your brain do what it needs to do!

Executive Function: Say What? 🤔

Executive Functioning is a group of brain skills that help us manage our learning, our time, our materials, and ourselves.

👑 Think of these skills as your brain's CEO!

Just like a CEO manages a big company, your Executive Functions manage your focus, actions, and decisions.
