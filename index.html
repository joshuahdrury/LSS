Spotlight: Serena Williams & Emotional Control 🎾

The Power of Executive Functioning Under Pressure

Serena Williams is widely considered one of the greatest tennis players in history, winning 23 Grand Slam singles titles. But tennis isn't just about physical strength—it requires immense executive functioning.

When playing in front of thousands of screaming fans, if Serena lost a point, her brain's CEO had to immediately activate Impulse Control and Emotional Regulation. Instead of getting frustrated, she relied on strict court routines, deep breathing, and focusing on the very next point.

"I really think mental toughness is your biggest weapon," Serena once shared. She kept a small black notebook in her tennis bag containing written reminders and tactical goals to keep her prefrontal cortex focused during stressful matches.
